Our Story

A little piece of home, on Orchard Street

Afri Spice opened its doors to bring the authentic tastes of Africa and the Caribbean to Weston-super-Mare. Whether you're after the perfect ingredients for jollof rice, ripe plantains, or hard-to-find pantry staples, we've built our shelves around what our community actually cooks at home.

We're proud to serve not just Weston-super-Mare, but families travelling in from Clevedon, Burnham-on-Sea, Bridgwater, Nailsea, Bristol and Portishead — because good food is worth the trip.
